About Stackly

From a two-person garage startup to a platform moving billions in cargo — this is the story of Stackly.

0 Years Moving
Our Story

Born at a dock, built for the world.

Stackly started in 2014 when two freight planners got tired of rebuilding the same spreadsheet every morning. What began as an ETA calculator is now an operating system for 5,000+ businesses.

Today our network touches 340 ports, 190 countries and over 24,000 shipments a day — yet the mission hasn't changed: make global logistics feel effortless.

We are engineers, planners and former dock workers, united by one belief — the flow of goods should never be the bottleneck of ambition.

Relentless Reliability

We treat every shipment like it carries something irreplaceable. 98% on-time isn't a slogan — it's an engineering target.

Intelligence First

Every feature starts with a question: what will the AI see before a human can? Prediction beats reaction, every time.

Sustainable by Default

Logistics moves 8% of global emissions. We build the tools that shrink that number — without shrinking your margins.

Mission & Vision

What drives us every day.

Our Mission

Make every supply chain self-driving.

We exist so that no business ever loses a customer because a container was late. Planning, sourcing, moving, tracking, delivering — all of it on autopilot, with humans in command.

Our Vision

A world where distance costs nothing.

We imagine a future where a maker in Nairobi competes on equal footing with one in New York — because logistics simply works, silently, invisibly, everywhere.
